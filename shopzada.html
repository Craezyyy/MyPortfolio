Business question

How can e-commerce records be organized so teams can explore sales, customer, and product performance more consistently?

Analysis & approach

I contributed to ETL pipelines in Apache Airflow handling over 1 million records, a Kimball-style dimensional model in PostgreSQL, and analytical SQL queries. The project also supported Power BI dashboard integration.

What this work provides

The work created a structured reporting foundation for sales, customer, and product analysis. I have not published a measured business outcome, so this case study focuses on the pipeline and data model rather than invented gains.
