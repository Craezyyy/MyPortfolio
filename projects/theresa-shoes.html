1. The Problem & Client Background

Ma. Theresa Shoes (Vergara Shoe Manufacturing) is a heritage footwear brand founded in Marikina City, known for handcrafted made-to-order (MTO) corporate footwear and custom bank orders (e.g., BDO, BPI). Despite superior craftsmanship, the business relied entirely on physical index cards, manual pen-and-paper measurement slips, and fragmented Excel records.

This traditional process created major business bottlenecks: lost client measurement profiles, delayed order turnaround, difficult manual payment reconciliation (installments, downpayments, uncollected balances), and zero online visibility against mass-produced competitors.

2. System Architecture & Role-Based Flow

As Project Developer, I helped design and implement an end-to-end system accommodating three distinct user personas:

  • Guest User (Public Showcase): Digital style catalog showcasing available silhouettes, leather finishes (Helga, Patent, Snake, Tanya), and company heritage with zero pricing ambiguity.
  • Repeat Customer (Bespoke Customizer): Allows authenticated users to configure custom shoes (specifying shoe mold type, heel height, heel shape, size, buckle, platform, and slingback toggles), generating an automated Order Summary PDF for streamlined verification.
  • Administrator / Owner Portal: A protected dashboard enabling the non-tech-savvy client to manage catalog items, track employee orders per corporate client, monitor installments/balances, and analyze revenue trends.

4. Key Engineering Deliverables

  • Interactive Spec Customizer: Built dynamic attribute configuration forms with live previews for shoe materials, molds, heel shapes, and color swatches.
  • Automated Order Generation: Implemented a client-side PDF export system generating complete custom measurement sheets formatted for direct production.
  • Executive Analytics & A/R Tracking: Built dashboard visualizations for Year-to-Date sales, total orders, and an uncollected balance tracker with corporate client filtering.
  • Catalog Content Management: Integrated administrative CRUD capabilities allowing immediate updates to product pricing, descriptions, and multi-image uploads.
