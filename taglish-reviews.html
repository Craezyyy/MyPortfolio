Business question

Can a language model detect dissatisfaction in Taglish e-commerce reviews, including concerns that may be missed by a simple positive/negative label?

Analysis & approach

Our team curated and annotated more than 10,000 Taglish reviews. We are fine-tuning XLM-RoBERTa and benchmarking it against BERT-based baselines. The next stage adds categories for specific customer concerns and evaluates how reliably the model recognizes them.

What this work provides

This is ongoing research. We have a curated dataset and model workflow, but final category-level results and business recommendations are still being validated. The intended use is to help teams spot recurring complaints and prioritize follow-up.
